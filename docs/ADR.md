# ADR｜Architecture Decision Record

## ADR-001
Strategic decoding belongs to Enterprise, not Interview.

## ADR-002
Interview must inherit Enterprise north-star metrics and core capabilities.

## ADR-003
Scenario / AI Opportunity can aggregate evidence from multiple Interviews.

## ADR-004
IndustryTemplate only provides default hypotheses. All such content must be marked as to_be_confirmed.

## ADR-005
One Page Card is not a standalone primary business object; it is the presentation form of AIOpportunityCard.

## ADR-006
Process flow must be backed by ProcessTwin.

## ADR-007
Next Best Move is the highest-priority live interview interaction.

## ADR-008
Stage-level or slot-level confirmation summary should bind to Slot, not appear as a global right-side module.

## ADR-009
Confirmed recommended questions should enter the left-side Q&A flow as pending-answer questions.

## ADR-010
Case Intelligence must be embedded in Interview Mission Room, not isolated as a primary page.

## ADR-011
Interview is raw material, not AI demand itself.

## ADR-012
AIOpportunityCard is derived from Interview Evidence and carries One Page Card structure.

## ADR-013
One Interview can generate multiple AIOpportunityCards; an AIOpportunityCard can later reference multiple Interviews.
