# PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

1. The product is an AI-native Mission System, not a traditional multi-page SaaS.
2. AI should maintain task state, detect deviation, recommend next action, and help generate deliverables.
3. Consultant field work should be driven by Next Best Move.
4. The user should not have to search for the next page or input from a blank state.
5. IndustryTemplate provides default hypotheses; Enterprise StrategicContext calibrates them.
6. StrategicContext belongs to Enterprise.
7. Interview is raw data, not the final AI demand.
8. AIOpportunityCard is the AI demand / opportunity derived from Interview Evidence.
9. One Page Card is the expression form of AIOpportunityCard.
10. ProcessTwin is the structured source of process truth.
11. Every conclusion must have source and confirmation status.
